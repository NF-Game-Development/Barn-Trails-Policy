Help us help you faster

Please include the following in your message:

  • Player ID: find this in-game under Settings → Account.
  • Device model and Android version.
  • A short description of the issue, and the level number if it happened during play.
  • Screenshots or a screen recording, if you have them.

Purchases

Purchases such as Remove Ads are processed by Google Play. If a purchase didn't arrive, include your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and appears in your purchase receipt email) along with your Player ID.
